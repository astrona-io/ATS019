# Prerequisites

- `kubectl` 1.35 on your `PATH`.
- A single-node `kind` cluster on Kubernetes 1.35. The lab creates it. No add-ons are needed.
- The namespaces `starfleet` and `outpost`, the CRD `teamenvironments.platform.starfleet.io` with a weak schema, and the `TeamEnvironment` `alpha` in `outpost`. The lab creates them.
- A text editor and a new, empty folder for your files.
